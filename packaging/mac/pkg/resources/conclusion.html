Installed

Open Huntsman for Mac from Applications or Spotlight.

The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for malicious software, because it isn't signed by an Apple developer account. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

The key tester and typing reactions ask for Input Monitoring; sound-reactive lighting asks for the Microphone.

A copy installed before with install.sh is now in the Trash; your settings and presets are kept. To remove the app later, open it and choose Keyboard, then Uninstall….
