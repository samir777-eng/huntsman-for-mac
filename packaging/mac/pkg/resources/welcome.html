Huntsman for Mac

An unofficial, open-source control app for the Razer Huntsman V3 Pro: actuation, Rapid Trigger, Snap Tap, lighting and Lighting Studio.

This puts Huntsman for Mac in your Applications folder. It sets up its background lighting service the first time you open it.

If you installed it before with install.sh, that copy in your own Applications folder is moved to the Trash. Your settings and presets are kept.
